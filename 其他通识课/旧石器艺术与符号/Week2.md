## Academic Sources

Peer-reviewed publications
Academic journals. (specific topics)
- Impact Factor (IF)
- Higher citation, higher IF is more prestigious
- Most prestigious : **Cell, Science, Nature** (IF 45.1, 47.3, 56.1)
"Predatory journals"
- Not real peer-reviewed journals.
Peer-review process
- Preprint online
- May take multiple years
- (A) submit (B) Journal editor reject or invite reviewers. (C) The reviewers evaluate the paper. (D) Editor reads reviews and decides:
  - Accept as-is.
  - Major revisions required.
  - Minor revisions required.
  - Reject.
- (E) Co-authors complete revisions
